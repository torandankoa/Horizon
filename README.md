Introduction
Horizon is a comprehensive Business-to-Consumer (B2C) e-commerce system focusing on the niche market of Gaming gear and Tactical-style accessories. The project was built with the goal of not only creating a unique shopping interface but also integrating real-world operational strategies: from payment security and SEO optimization to data-driven logistics management.
Slogan: "Walk Past The Horizon."
Strategic Core Features
1. Operations & Payment (E-commerce Core)
VnPay Protocol Integration: Integrated with the national payment gateway, automating verification processes, real-time stock deduction, and transaction logging (Transactions).
Inventory & Logistics Management: A Product Receipts system records cost prices and procurement history, allowing for precise net profit calculations.
Cart System (Cargo Manifest): Utilizes Session JSON Serialization to optimize performance and store complex data objects.
2. Digital Marketing & SEO Optimization
SEO-Friendly URLs (Slugs): Automated generation of keyword-rich slugs from product names, optimized for Google search engines.
Social Marketing: Implemented Open Graph (OG Tags) to ensure professional display of images, prices, and descriptions when shared on Facebook/Zalo.
Email Marketing: Integrated Mailchimp for Lead Generation and executing "Intel Briefing" newsletter campaigns.
3. User Experience & CRM
QRF Support (Livechat): Embedded Tawk.to for 24/7 real-time customer support.
Behavioral Analytics: Utilizes Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to monitor traffic flow and optimize the sales funnel.
Modern Light Interface: Minimalist design focused on products, ensuring fast response times (< 2s).

System Architecture
The project applies the Areas structure to decouple Administrative and Customer business logic:
Area/Admin: Command Center (Dashboard, Revenue Statistics, Inventory Management, Order Status Updates).
Area/Customer: Online Storefront (Search, Sidebar Filters, Secure Checkout Workflow).
Infrastructure: Contains advanced helper modules (SlugHelper, VnPayLibrary, SessionExtensions).
